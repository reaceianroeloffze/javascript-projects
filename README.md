### [Random Background Colour Generator](https://github.com/reaceianroeloffze/javascript-projects-portfolio/tree/Root/random-background-colour-generator)

In this project, I created a random colour generator that generates a random colour every time the user clicks the button.
The colour is then displayed in the background of the page.
The colour is also displayed in the text box in hexadecimal format.
As the colour is generated, the text box is updated with the new colour.

[Random Background Colour Generator—Live Demo](https://reaceianroeloffze.github.io/javascript-projects/random-background-colour-generator/)

### [Guess My Number](https://github.com/reaceianroeloffze/javascript-projects/tree/Root/guess-my-number)

This is a game where you have to guess a random chosen number between one and another number. Your starting score is the same as the highest number in the range of guesses you make. Each incorrect guess decreases your score by 1. If you succeed in guessing the right number, your highest score is stored.

[Guess My Number—Live Demo](https://reaceianroeloffze.github.io/javascript-projects/guess-my-number/)

### [Pig Game]()

A game for two players. You take turns rolling a die. The number of the die determines your current score. Your score accumulates as you roll on. You can hold your score if you like. Choosing to hold your score ends your turn and resets your accumulated score to 0. The held score goes to your overall score. Should you roll a 1 without holding, your turn also ends and your current accumulated score is also reset. Typically, the first player to reach 100 points wins the game.
